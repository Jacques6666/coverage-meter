# A DEBT OF YEARS — Book 2 Outline

**Target length:** about 93,000 words (a prologue, 30 chapters and an epilogue).
**POV:** Dual. Tam in first person past (as in Book 1). Severin in close third person past (as in the Book 1 epilogue). Each chapter opens with the POV character's name. The prologue is Maren and the epilogue is Aurelia, both in close third.
**Timeline:** Six days after the Reckoning, through the Renewal at the Turning of the Year (the spring equinox, about five weeks later), plus a week of aftermath.
**Book 2 question:** *Can Tam and Severin free Maren from under the clock before Aurelia binds Tam there beside her forever?*
**Book 2 answer:** Yes, but the price is Lotte Varga's life, and cutting the Stay starts the clock walking toward midnight. When it strikes twelve, every year ever bought, stolen or given goes home. Kit's Grace is given years.

---

## How the clock works (new rules this book establishes)

- **The Horologe was built to give.** The first wells designed it to share their overflow across the city as every child's Allotment. The Founders turned it into an engine that only takes.
- **The Stay.** A braided cord of the well's own silver thread runs from the ring around the chained well's throat up the pendulum shaft to the escapement. It holds the hand at eleven. It is tied with the **Keeper's Knot**, which the Keeper reties every **Turning of the Year** (the **Renewal**) with her own blood while her palm lies over the well's heart. The Keeper skims from the well through the Stay; that is how Keepers live for centuries.
- **The Lines.** Other silver threads run from the well sideways through the chamber walls and out to the great houses of the **Inner Ledger**, the oldest Long-Lived families, who drink from the well directly.
- **Slips.** When the well's time flows hard to and from a well of her own blood (Tam), the Stay shivers and the hand slips one minute before catching again. It slipped to 11:01 at the end of Book 1. In this book it slips to 11:02 (Ch. 7) and 11:03 (Ch. 17). Every slip makes the Gilt's counts *flicker* at night, but nothing is lost while the Stay holds.
- **Aurelia's plan:** at the Renewal, bind Tam under the clock beside Maren and retie the Stay through both of them. Two wells, one Stay, and it never slips again.
- **Cutting the Stay** (from the Founders' Charter): "The Stay may be cut only in the slack of the Well's heart, and the cut shall be paid in full by the hand that makes it." Whoever cuts it gives their whole count to the cut.
- **After the cut,** the hand walks one minute each day toward twelve. As it walks, one sixtieth of every debt of years drains home each day. The Gilt's counts begin to shrink.
- **Midnight, the Settlement** (Charter page eleven, which Vadra hides): "...every hour shall return to whence it came: the bought to the seller or the seller's blood, the stolen to the robbed, the given to the giver, and the spent to none."
  - **Who loses:** the Long-Lived die. Kit (30 years of Grace) drops back toward his natural count. Rook (paid in years) and Vadra (living on her late husband's years) die. Severin loses every year Tam ever gave him; burned years are "spent to none."
  - **Who gains:** Bry gets back the 56 years Lysander stole, now in Aurelia. Hollis gets the Penhallow years his ancestor sold. The whole Wick gets its families' sold years back.

## Thread-speech (new)

- A well can speak along a thread: a thought pushed on a breath of time. Maren speaks to Tam this way through the mother's tether. She teaches Tam, who can then speak along any thread on her wrist (Severin's silver, Bry's black). Tam pays a minute a word, and it refills.
- Once a well has opened a thread, the other end can answer, at **an hour a word**. Severin rations his words. "I love you" costs him three hours.
- Aurelia cannot hear it. She can feel Tam's pulse change when Tam speaks.

## PROLOGUE — Maren, ten years ago
The February night. Maren Fenn knows the Ledger's men are coming; she sold where she shouldn't have. She nurses three-week-old Kit and sets him in the cot. She sits on the end of nine-year-old Tamsin's bed and ties the mother's tether her own mother taught her, freezing Tam's count at 71 years, 4 months. She means to come back for Kit and tie him too. She kisses Tam's forehead ("Mind the baby") and walks into the fog to lead the men away from the house. Under the lamps at the bottom of Tallow Lane, a small woman in black lace is waiting, eating something sweet.

## PART ONE — THE GILDED CAGE

### 1. Breakfast with the Keeper (Tam)
Thane House, six days after the Reckoning. Tam lives in the Violet Rooms in the west wing, unchained, watched, with a terrified Wick maid, Nessa, and four threads on her wrist (Severin's silver, Bry's black, Aurelia's Ledger thread, and her mother's invisible one). Every morning at breakfast in the Glass Room, Aurelia drinks a year from Tam over tea while Tam refills from Maren: "My count never moved, and that was the horror of it. It made me the perfect cup." Across the river the clock reads one minute past eleven, and the Gilt can't stop looking at it. "Where would you go, dear? Every year you have comes from your mother, and your mother is mine."
**Hook:** That night, through the mother's tether: *Tamsin. I can teach you to speak to him. But learn fast. At the Turning she means to bring you down to me, and keep you.*

### 2. The Laundress (Severin)
Gannet's back room, with 4 years 2 months on his arm. The Unwound need someone who can cut a Ledger thread without killing both ends. The only person in the Wick who ever has is **Lotte Varga**, laundress in the Sump steam-houses and Liesl's mother. Severin goes to her. She slaps him, then makes him kneel on the wet stone among the lye vats. Her price: "You'll learn from me, Thane. And every day you do, you'll hear about my girl."
**Hook:** She looks at his arm. "Four years. Then we'd better start tonight."

### 3. Thread-Speech (Tam)
Night lessons from her mother. Maren tells of the line of wells, how the crooked finger comes when a well wakes ("You'll know if it ever comes to you; your finger will tell you"), and Kit, whom she never got back to. By day, Aurelia shows Tam the Gallery of the Outlived: two centuries of portraits of everyone she has buried, and a glimpse of her loneliness. A grey woman flees at the end of a corridor.
**Hook:** Tam sends her first word down the silver thread. *Severin.* Across the city, his heart jumps.

### 4. One Word (Severin)
He hears her in the laundry and staggers into a vat. Lotte: "That's well-speech. Answer and it'll cost you an hour a word." He answers: *Here.* First lessons in cutting: every thread has a **slack**, the instant between two heartbeats when nothing moves; cut in the slack and both ends live, cut on the beat and you kill the weaker. They practice on the black threads of Wick folk indentured to brokers, the Unwound's real work. Kit, at the map table, wants to know when he can see Tam.
**Hook:** Hollis arrives from the Gilt: "Grandmother Thane's holding a salon tomorrow. She's *showing* her."

### 5. The Salon (Tam)
Aurelia's salon for the Inner Ledger. Tam sits in white on a dais while Aurelia draws "wellwater" through her into crystal vials and the Long-Lived drink. They are frightened: since the minute, their counts flicker at night. **Lady Ismay Lisle** (190) eyes Tam like a purchase. Hollis is there in plum and passes Tam a pastry: "Pell made it. Eat the note." The note: *We're coming. Learn the house.*
**Hook:** Aurelia announces the Renewal at the Turning of the Year: "The Stay will be retied. And this year it will be tied with her."

### 6. The Wick Telling (Severin)
Lotte tells the laundresses' version of the Drowning: the clock was made to give, and the Founders stopped it at eleven so the giving would never come round. "The rich stopped the clock so they'd never have to go home. It isn't a joke, Thane. It's a *recipe*." Nobody alive knows how the Stay is tied; the answer is in the **Founders' Charter**, in the vault under the Ledger House. Sergeant **Kell Durrow** (the red-faced Greycoat) raids the steam-house. Severin casts a stilling over the whole room to get the women out (4 years 2 months down to 3 years 2 months).
**Hook:** Lotte looks at his arm. "Three years. For a room full of washerwomen." A pause. "You stupid boy."

### 7. The Leak (Tam)
Tam feels his year go. At night she pushes five years into him while he sleeps. The flow from Maren through Tam to Severin shakes Thane House, and the clock slips to **11:02**. At dawn Aurelia notices the yield was short. Tam lies: "The clock took it." Tam finds out that Nessa reports on her because Aurelia holds Nessa's little sister's Grace hostage. Tam makes Nessa an offer only a well's daughter can make, and wins an ally.
**Hook:** Aurelia: "If you leak again, darling, I'll find where it goes. And I'll drink him."

### 8. Eight Years (Severin)
He wakes with 8 years 2 months and knows exactly who did it. He sends one word: *Don't.* She sends a paragraph back, ending: *You don't get to decide that.* He laughs until he can't breathe, and Lotte says, "She loves you, you fool, and you're making her sneak." **Orrin Fisk** arrives from Calder Bay with a coil of rope ("I owe you four years"). The heist crew forms: Wat Sorrel knows the Ledger House archive, Hollis has a Council seat on Council day, plus Bry, Pell and Orrin.
**Hook:** "There are passages in Thane House," Severin says, "that my grandmother has forgotten I know."

## PART TWO — THE CHARTER

### 9. Evadne (Tam)
Night wandering in Thane House. In the music room Tam meets the grey woman: **Lady Evadne Thane**, Severin's mother, alive, frail and full of guilt. Aurelia drained Severin's father **Aldric** when Severin was seven. Evadne gave up her son to the tower to keep him alive, and has haunted the west corridor ever since. She tells Tam about the passages Aldric taught the boy.
**Hook:** Tam returns to the Violet Rooms. In the dark, a panel in the wall slides open.

### 10. Through the Walls (Severin)
The reunion: her arm, the cold Ledger thread on it, his rage, her laugh. They kiss, and the night is theirs (closed door). They trade plans: the Charter, the Renewal, the house. For the first time he takes years from her without being tricked: "Ours."
**Hook:** A knock. "Tamsin, dear? I couldn't sleep. Let's have chocolate." Severin slides back behind the panel a breath too late, and Aurelia sniffs the air: "Do I smell clock oil?"

### 11. Chocolate at Midnight (Tam)
Aurelia sits on the bed in her nightgown while Severin hides behind the panel, and talks. She was born fifty-three years after the Drowning; her mother was Keeper before her. She is afraid of midnight: "Do you know what happens at midnight, dear? *Everyone goes home.*" She makes her offer: help retie the Stay and Tam will be Keeper after her, never dying, never ending. Tam is tempted (Kit, forever). Aurelia leaves.
**Hook:** In the morning, workmen are bricking up the old passages of Thane House.

### 12. Never Again the Well (Severin)
The heist. It is Council day at the Ledger House. Hollis takes Penhallow's seat (to his mother's horror), Bry and Pell carry trays, Wat works the archive, Orrin handles the ropes, and Severin comes in through the Founders' tunnels under the Gilt. They reach the vault under the three-foot gold letters of *Never again the well*.
**Hook:** The vault is already open. Someone else is inside.

### 13. The Vault (Severin)
Lady Lisle's agents are stealing the Charter for their own leverage. A fight in the dark among the Founders' relics; they get the Charter. Durrow's Greycoats arrive. Pell holds a doorway so the others can run.
**Hook:** Pell, singing Anni's hymn, goes down under six grey coats.

### 14. Pell (Tam)
Durrow brings Pell to Thane House. At breakfast Aurelia drains him in front of Tam to make her talk: "Where is my Charter, dear?" Tam doesn't know. Pell sings. Then **Evadne** stands between Pell and the Keeper, the first time in fourteen years she has defied her: "Enough, Grandmother." Aurelia slaps her and has Pell taken to the cellars alive, as leverage.
**Hook:** Tam sends down the silver thread: *They have Pell.*

### 15. The Founders' Charter (Severin)
In Gannet's back room, Wat reads the archaic script aloud: the Horologe, the Allotment, the Stay, the Keeper's Knot, the Renewal, and *the cut shall be paid in full by the hand that makes it*, then the hand walking a minute a day to the Settlement. Lotte goes quiet, and Severin knows what she has decided. Vadra says *let it strike*; Severin says *we save them first*.
**Hook:** The pages are numbered. Page eleven is missing.

### 16. The Cellars (Tam)
The rescue of Pell from inside, with Tam, Evadne and Nessa inside the house and Severin and Bry through the one passage the masons missed. It works. Severin begs Tam to come too. She can't: "If I run, she takes it out of my mother." They part in the dark.
**Hook:** Aurelia, at the top of the cellar stairs with a lamp: "Well. If you won't stay where I put you, dear, I'll put you somewhere you will."

### 17. Two Wells (Tam)
Punishment: a night bound beside Maren under the clock. Face to face with her mother for the first time since she was nine: talk, tears, laughter, her mother's hands. Tam learns about Kit's birth, the Marrow line, and how the well wakes. Through the long night they push time back and forth between them without meaning to.
**Hook:** Dawn. Aurelia comes down the Keeper's stair and looks up the shaft. The hand reads **11:03**. "Interesting," says the Keeper.

## PART THREE — THE TURNING

### 18. Three Minutes (Severin)
The city at three minutes past eleven. The Gilt's counts flicker by day now. Gilt nobles are buying years on the Row at ten times the price, and brokers are rich. The Council meets in panic. The Unwound fix their plan on the Renewal. Lotte tells Severin about Liesl as a child: the cards, the swearing, the watch. He listens and grieves with her.
**Hook:** "When the time comes, Thane, it'll be my hand on the Stay. Not yours." "No." "I wasn't asking."

### 19. Lady Lisle (Tam)
At the Gilt's Thaw Regatta, Ismay Lisle offers Tam escape in exchange for being Lisle's well. Tam plays along to learn how the Renewal works (the Keeper's blood, the palm over the well's heart, the knot tied in the slack) and learns Lisle plans her own coup in the chamber.
**Hook:** Aurelia knew all along. "Ismay has been trying to steal my wells for a hundred years. Did you say yes, dear?"

### 20. Fire on Broker's Row (Severin)
Hollis's mother has engaged him to Ambrose Vey, which Hollis doesn't hate. Bry sits with Pell while he recovers. Severin and Hollis talk, really talk, about dying young. Then Durrow raids: Gannet's shop burns, Gannet escapes through the cellar, and **Kit is taken**.
**Hook:** Kit's tin watch lies in the ash on the floor of the back room, still ticking.

### 21. Kit (Tam)
Aurelia brings Kit to Thane House: "At the Renewal you'll lie down beside your mother of your own will, or I'll drink your brother's Grace in front of you." She gives them one night together, out of cruel generosity. Tam tells Kit their mother is alive.
**Hook:** Kit, very small in the big Violet bed: "Then I'll see her." "Yes."

### 22. The Captain and the Steward (Severin)
Severin goes to Captain Marr, who has had enough. Marr's sister was a tithe. Rook will open the cistern door with her keys. Orrin's ropes will take Severin down the pendulum shaft from his old tower room into the chamber from above. The plan, piece by piece: Marr's men take the Keeper's stair; the Unwound come through the cisterns; Severin drops down the shaft, cuts Tam's Ledger thread in the slack and holds Aurelia; Lotte cuts the Stay; Bry gets Kit. This time Severin lets Tam carry part of it: she'll feed him years through their thread while he fights.
**Hook:** Vadra, privately: "When the hand starts walking, Thane, don't let anyone stop it. Whatever it costs."

### 23. Three Words (Tam)
The night before. A long whispered conversation down the silver thread across the dark city, funny and tender. He stops rationing. "I love you" costs him three hours: "Cheapest thing I ever bought." Evadne gives Tam Aldric Thane's ring for her son.
**Hook:** Dawn. Aurelia at the door, in white: "Today, dear, you go home to your mother."

## PART FOUR — THE RENEWAL

### 24. The Keeper's Stair (Tam)
The procession down the Keeper's stair: the Council of Houses, the Inner Ledger, Lady Lisle, Durrow holding Kit, Evadne in grey. The chamber, the pendulum, Maren under it, and now visible, the **Stay**, a braided silver cord rising from her throat-ring into the dark. Aurelia begins binding Tam beside her mother: wrists, ankles, throat. The Long-Lived kneel in a ring with their threads, exactly like the engraving.
**Hook:** Aurelia raises the Keeper's knife to open her palm. Through the silver thread, Severin says: *Now.*

### 25. The Shaft (Severin)
The same hour, from above: his tower room, the grate pried up, Orrin's rope, six hundred feet down the pendulum shaft in the dark with the great disc hanging below him. Rook's keys and the Unwound in the cisterns. Marr's men on the Keeper's stair.
**Hook:** He lets go of the rope and drops into the ring of the Long-Lived.

### 26. The Slack (Tam)
The battle. Severin stills the ring of the Long-Lived while Tam pours years into him down the thread ("ours"). He opens the silver door from inside and the Unwound pour in. Marr's Greycoats fight Durrow's. Hollis stills the Keeper's knife for a heartbeat at the cost of a month. Evadne drives a hairpin into Durrow's hand, and Bry gets Kit, finally landing the punch she's been saving. Severin cuts Tam's Ledger thread in the slack and the cold heartbeat leaves her. Lisle grabs for Tam. Aurelia draws on her own Long-Lived to fuel herself, and they wither where they kneel.
**Hook:** Lotte Varga lays her hand on the Stay.

### 27. Paid in Full (Severin)
Lotte cuts the Stay in the slack of Maren's heart. The cut takes her whole count. The Stay parts with a sound like the biggest string in the world snapping. The hand above lurches, the pendulum swings, and every bell in Lanthorn rings. The Lines burn away through the walls, and Maren's threads fall from her. Lotte dies in Severin's arms. Her last word is the name only Liesl ever called him: "Sev."
**Hook:** All around the chamber, gold begins to bleed out of the Long-Lived like smoke, including the Keeper. Aurelia flees up her stair with her count shrinking.

### 28. Mama (Tam)
Maren is free and falls into Tam's arms. Pell carries her out through the cisterns, singing. In the Tithe Hall, Kit sees his mother for the first time since he was three weeks old. "You drew this room," Maren says. It is the emotional peak of the book. Durrow is in chains. Lisle and Aurelia are gone.
**Hook:** Rook looks at her own arm in the lamplight, says nothing, and pulls her sleeve down.

### 29. Page Eleven (Severin)
The city at 11:04, one minute a day. Lotte's funeral among the laundresses of the Sump. Evadne and her son, after fourteen years. Severin confronts Vadra, who hands him page eleven: *the bought to the seller or the seller's blood, the stolen to the robbed, the given to the giver, and the spent to none.* He understands what it means for Kit, Rook, Vadra and himself. Then he does the thing he never did in Book 1: he goes straight to tell Tam.
**Hook:** He is walking up Tallow Lane with the page in his pocket.

### 30. Fifty-Four Days (Tam)
Tallow Lane on the third evening after the cut (11:06). The family together. The Wick is celebrating: old women watching sold years come home up their arms, and Hollis is delirious because his count is *growing* as the Penhallow years return. Joy. Severin comes to the door with his face set, and before he can speak, Kit comes in from the street holding out his arm.
**Final line:** *"Tam? My count's getting shorter."*

## EPILOGUE — Aurelia
Somewhere under the Gilt, in the Founders' tunnels, the Keeper watches her own count shrink a little more with each day, with Ismay Lisle beside her. "Fifty-four days, Ismay." They will stop the clock the way the Founders did, with a well. "Maren Fenn is spent. But the girl..." The girl will learn what midnight costs the people she loves. "She'll come down those stairs on her own two feet, to save the boy with the clocks. They always come for family."
