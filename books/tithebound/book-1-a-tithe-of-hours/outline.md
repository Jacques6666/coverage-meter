# A TITHE OF HOURS — Book 1 Outline

**Target length:** about 95,000 words (30 chapters of about 3,100 words, plus an epilogue).
**Timeline:** Tithe Day (first day of autumn) → the Long Night (midwinter) → the Reckoning (end of winter).
**Book 1 question:** *Who is draining the tithes, and why?*
**Book 1 answer:** Lysander, on Aurelia's orders. Aurelia is hunting the person secretly receiving half of her captive wellspring's time: Tam.

---

## ACT ONE — Bought (Ch. 1–8)

### 1. The Tithe Market
Before dawn, Tam inks ten years off her count by candlelight. Her secret: her count has read 71 years, 4 months since she was nine. Kit, who has one year left, critiques her inkwork and gives her his map of the Meridian ("where the clock keeps its heart"). At the Meridian forecourt under the stopped clock, the novices inspect the Wick candidates like livestock. Bry is bought by Lysander Crane. Hollis apologizes to everyone. A binder's one-minute reading dims Tam's top notch, and it relights. Severin Thane arrives late and bids an unheard-of **thirty years** of Grace, naming Kit by his full name. During the threading he leans in.
**Hook:** *"Your count moved, Miss Fenn. I watched it go up."*

### 2. The Tithe Hall
Steward Rook's rules: grey uniforms, the eleventh bell, the Seventhday weighing, "Do not go below the Hall." Tam meets the tithes: Joss (66, in love with Hetty back home), Anni (63, sings), Pell (49, bakes), and Bry, bitter and suspicious ("Nobody pays thirty for the same thing"). The gossip about Severin's dead tithes. Under her blanket Tam checks the binder's minute; it's back. The threading smeared her ink, and she has about two weeks of ink left. The tether hums with a second pulse that won't let her sleep.
**Hook:** Steward Rook at the door: *"The Thane heir wants his tithe. Now."*

### 3. A Room Full of Clocks
Severin's tower room, directly beneath the dead clockworks, crowded with hundreds of small ticking clocks he repairs by hand. A tense, funny negotiation. He thinks she's an ink-cheat hiding a high count, which makes her the perfect partner. **The deal:** whenever he casts in public, her count must appear to fall by the cost of the working. She'll ink it, and he will never draw on her. Tam's terms: he never draws, not one minute, and if she's caught, Kit keeps the Grace. He agrees too fast. He knows Kit's lungs and their address. She notices his gloves never come off.
**Hook:** *"One more thing, Fenn. Don't go near the cisterns at night."*

### 4. First Draw
Tethering class with Magister Vadra: the first public draw. Lysander drains a month from Bry with a smile, and she faints. Hollis refuses to draw on Pell and is humiliated, then cracks a joke that makes Tam like him at once. Severin performs a flawless stilling, and Tam fakes the drop live with ink on her fingertip and her sleeve: the first high-wire scene. Vadra's eyes linger on Tam's arm. That night, the alarm bell: a tithe is missing.
**Hook:** Joss Mallory's bed is empty, and his boots are still under it.

### 5. Blank
Joss is pulled from the cistern, his count completely dark, drained to the last minute. The official verdict is that he sold his years to a smuggled-in broker. Tam, the counter, sees what the Greycoats miss: the drain happened in one pull, which is impossible without a great working; his thread was cut with a Gilt-style knot; and his count was 66, the second-highest in the Hall. Severin arrives, looks at the body, and goes white.
**Hook:** As Severin turns away, Tam sees a dark stain on his glove.

### 6. The Clock That Stopped
In the library, Hollis adopts Tam: banter, his 6-year count, Penhallow poverty. He tells the official history: the Drowning of the wellsprings, the clock stuck at eleven. Tam remembers her mother telling the same story differently. Bry has bruises from Lysander. Tam storms up to the tower to accuse Severin of Joss's murder. They have a vicious argument, and she spots a broken pocket watch engraved *L*. (Liesl).
**Hook:** *"If I were draining tithes, Fenn, I'd have started with the one who lies to me."*

### 7. The Chancellor's Address
Lady Aurelia Thane comes to the Meridian: candied violets, perfect warmth, pure menace. She inspects the tithes' wrists and lingers on Tam's: "Seventy-one— forgive me, *sixty-one*. What a generous count for a Wick girl." (A slip, or a test?) Severin steps between them, the first protective act. Tam learns that the Gilt considers showing a count vulgar, and that Severin's cuff never comes off.
**Hook:** Aurelia to Severin: *"Your last tithe lasted four months. Let's see if this one lasts the winter."*

### 8. Black Market
Tam's ink is nearly gone. She sneaks over the Hours Bridge at night (forbidden) to Tallow Lane. Kit has his Grace, 31 years, in a joyful, tearful scene. At Mother Gannet's shop, Gannet sells her ink and warns her that the Greycoats are asking about high counts that don't drop. On the way back the bridge lamps go out, and someone grabs her. It's Severin, who followed her and covers for her with the bridge wardens.
**Hook:** *"You went to an inksmith. So you're not hiding a low count, Fenn. You're hiding a high one."*

## ACT TWO (A) — Partners (Ch. 9–15)

### 9. The Dial
A duel on the Dial: Severin against Lysander. Hastening, stillings, gold light. Tam must fake heavy drops in real time and falters when her ink runs. To protect her, Severin throws the duel and is humiliated in front of the whole college. Tam realizes he sacrificed his standing for her secret.
**Hook:** Lysander, low in her ear: *"When my cousin tires of you, little tithe, I'll be waiting."*

### 10. The Library of Echoes
Tam, Hollis and Bry investigate. Hollis offers to cast an Echo at the cistern; Tam refuses to let him spend his time. Instead they bribe a Ledger clerk's copyist for tithe records. The pattern: eleven tithes drained over ten years, every one with a count above 60. It began the year Tam's mother "went into the river."
**Hook:** *"Someone's hunting long counts. And mine is the longest in the Hall."*

### 11. Lessons
Severin insists on teaching her theory ("If you're going to lie about magic, learn it"). Late-night lessons in the clock room. He teaches her to feel the tether, and she senses something wrong with the flow, as if it runs from him rather than to him. Banter, attraction, hands not quite touching. He shows her the inside of a watch. His glove catches on a gear and slips.
**Hook:** Tam sees his bare wrist. *Nine years.* "You're dying."

### 12. Liesl
He tells her about Liesl, the girl he was made to drain at sixteen, whose count went out "like a row of candles." He has burned his own life ever since. Tam's hate cracks wide open. He turns it around on her: "Now you. Your real count, Tamsin." She lies. He knows she's lying and lets her.
**Hook:** The alarm bell again.

### 13. Anni
Anni (count 63) is found drained in the Gallery of Hours, beneath the portraits of the Long-Lived. Lockdown. Captain Marr's Greycoats interrogate the tithes. Tam was out of bounds and has the highest count, so she is the suspect. Severin gives her a public alibi ("She was with me all night"), and the scandal ignites.
**Hook:** Bry: *"Is it true? You're in his bed?"* Tam: *"No. I wish the reason were that simple."*

### 14. The Founders' Well
Vadra summons Tam and reveals that she knows about the ink: "I inked for two years." Vadra hints at the Unwound and shows Tam a three-hundred-year-old engraving of a wellspring chained beneath a clock. She warns: "Lady Thane has been looking for something for ten years. Pray you are not it."
**Hook:** The chained woman in the engraving has Tam's mother's hands, down to the crooked little finger.

### 15. Tallow Lane
A Greycoat has been to Tallow Lane asking Kit about their mother. Severin takes Tam out through a secret Thane passage under the river. He meets Kit and is gentle and awkward with him. Kit shows him his maps, including the Meridian with the spot under the clock. Tam falls a little and hates it.
**Hook:** Kit, to Severin: *"Mama said the clock is hungry. Is that true?"* Severin doesn't answer.

---

## ACT TWO (B) — The Long Night (Ch. 16–22)

### 16. Silk and Ink
Preparing for the Long Night, the midwinter ball at Thane House where tithes attend as their wrights' "shadows." Hollis dresses Tam (pure joy on the page). Bry confesses that Lysander has drawn her down from 58 to 31. Tam can't help her without a thread. She decides to tell Severin the truth after the ball.
**Hook:** Severin sees Tam in the dress and, for once, has nothing to say. Then: "Stay where I can see you tonight."

### 17. The Long Night
The Gilt in all its glittering horror: the Long-Lived, a fountain that runs with gold, years served as party favors. Aurelia's toast. Tam and Severin dance with the tether singing, and they almost kiss on a frozen balcony.
**Hook:** Every candle in Thane House goes out at once. Screaming.

### 18. Stilling
Masked attackers in the dark. Lysander tries to drag Tam away. Severin casts a stilling that freezes the entire ballroom, which is enormous and costs him years. Tam watches his life pouring out through the tether and instinctively pushes time *back* into him. He feels it: her count doesn't drop.
**Hook:** In the frozen ballroom, one figure still moves. Aurelia smiles and claps slowly.

### 19. Seventy-One Years, Four Months
In his rooms at Thane House, Severin confronts her: "You refill." She tells him everything: frozen since nine, the sales in Saltmarket, the fear. He understands exactly what his grandmother has been hunting. **First kiss**, desperate and furious. Afterward he tells her to leave the Meridian tonight. She refuses, because leaving forfeits Kit's Grace.
**Hook:** *"Then I'll have to make certain nobody ever looks at your count again."* He leaves without saying how.

### 20. Heir
Back at the Meridian, Severin is cold and distant. He has met Aurelia in private. Lysander gloats: "Did he tell you what Grandmother promised? The heirship, to whoever finds her well." Tam searches Severin's desk.
**Hook:** A list of every drained tithe for ten years, in Severin's handwriting.

### 21. The List
Confrontation. Severin says he has been tracking the murders for years to stop them and has no proof. He admits that after the ballroom he knew Tam was the one his grandmother hunts, and that he met Aurelia to steer her toward Orrin, "someone she can't reach." Tam can't tell whether he's protecting her or selling her. She walks out.
**Hook:** Pell, breathless: Bry hasn't come back to the Hall.

### 22. Below
Searching for Bry with Hollis and Pell. Lysander's rooms are empty. Tam remembers Severin's warning about the cisterns at night and Steward Rook's rule about going below the Hall. She follows the tether-sense of wrongness downward.
**Hook:** Tam wades into the black cistern water alone, toward a door that shouldn't exist.

---

## ACT THREE — The Reckoning (Ch. 23–30)

### 23. Black Thread
In the Undercroft passage Tam finds Bry chained, drained to two weeks. Lysander is gone. Hollis arrives. To save Bry, Tam uses Gannet's illegal black thread to tether herself to Bry and pours in twenty years. Bry lives. By the lamplight Hollis watches Tam's count climb back.
**Hook:** Hollis, whispering: *"Tam. Your count. It's going back up."*

### 24. Three Who Know
The friends regroup: Tam, Hollis, and Bry, still recovering. Hollis is joyful, then afraid for her, then furious when she offers him time ("Don't you dare offer me a single hour"). Bry names Lysander as her attacker, but she also heard a woman in the dark, *eating something sweet*.
**Hook:** The Reckoning is in three days. Every wright must perform a great working drawn from their tithe, in front of the Long-Lived, and Aurelia will be watching every tithe's count.

### 25. Orrin's Letter
Severin proves himself: a letter from Orrin, alive in the south, smuggled out by Severin last year. Reconciliation and a real confession. Severin's plan for the Reckoning: a "reversed draw" that burns his own count while appearing to drain Tam. It would leave him with about two years. Tam refuses. They fight because they love each other. Vadra offers a dangerous alternative.
**Hook:** *"Then we do it my way."*

### 26. Echo
They need proof of Lysander. Hollis insists on casting an Echo in the Undercroft passage to capture Lysander draining Bry, and he spends a month of his six years to do it, over Tam's protests: his heroic moment. They capture it in an echo-lens. Beyond the passage, a sealed silver door hums with time, and Tam's whole body pulls toward it.
**Hook:** From behind the silver door: a single, slow heartbeat that matches Tam's.

### 27. The Reckoning
The Dial, the Gallery packed with the Long-Lived, Aurelia presiding. Wrights perform their great workings. Lysander drains his new tithe brutally. Hollis refuses to draw on Pell and casts with his own time, and the tithes stand and applaud. Then it's Severin and Tam's turn.
**Hook:** Aurelia: *"Severin. Draw on her. All of it."*

### 28. There You Are
Severin refuses in front of the whole Gilt and breaks with his grandmother. Tam plays the echo-lens: Lysander draining Bry, and a woman's voice. Uproar. Aurelia, serene, drains Lysander dead in a single breath ("Justice is done") and pins every murder on him. In the struggle Tam's ink has smeared, and her count blazes at 71 years, 4 months.
**Hook:** Aurelia, softly: *"There you are."*

### 29. The Heart of the Clock
Severin attacks his grandmother. She stills him effortlessly and burns five years from him as punishment, leaving him with 4. Tam surrenders to save him and Kit. Aurelia takes her down through the silver door. Beneath the Horologe's great pendulum hangs a woman chained in silver thread, her count blazing endlessly. Aurelia: "Ten years she's fed me. And ten years something stole half of her. Now I know where it went."
**Hook:** The woman opens her eyes. *"Tamsin. I told you never to come here."*

### 30. Eleven O'Clock
Aurelia's terms: Tam will be tethered to Aurelia directly, as her new well. In exchange, Kit keeps his years and Severin keeps his life. Tam agrees. Through the old thread, her mother speaks to her for the first time in ten years: *"Not yet. Wait for him."* As Aurelia's binder threads Tam's wrist, the Meridian's clock, stopped at eleven for three hundred years, moves one minute. Everyone looks up.
**Final line:** *Somewhere above us, for the first time in three hundred years, the clock began to count.*

### Epilogue — Severin
In the Wick, with four years left, Severin walks into Mother Gannet's back room, where Vadra and the Unwound are waiting. "I need to learn how to cut a Ledger thread." "What do you want to steal from your grandmother, Thane?" "Everything she ever took." The door opens and Kit Fenn walks in with his map of the place under the clock.
