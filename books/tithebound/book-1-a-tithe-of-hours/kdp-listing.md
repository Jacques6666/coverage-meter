## Description

*(KDP lets you use bold and italics in the description box.)*

> **In the city of Lanthorn, your life is written on your wrist — and the rich are buying it by the year.**
>
> Tamsin Fenn has seventy-one years left to live.
>
> She's had seventy-one years left since she was nine.
>
> No matter how much time she spends, sells, or bleeds, by morning her count is whole again. In the Wick, where the poor sell their years for bread and die at forty, that secret is worth more than her life — and would cost her exactly that.
>
> So when her little brother's time begins to run out, Tam does the only thing she can. She paints her count down, steps onto the Tithe Market, and sells herself to the Meridian: the academy where the heirs of the Gilt learn to cast magic fueled by stolen years.
>
> Her buyer is **Severin Thane**. Cold, brilliant heir to the family that keeps the Ledger of every life in Lanthorn — and whose last tithe went home in a box. He paid more for her than anyone in history. He saw her count move. And he wants her help to lie.
>
> But tithes are being pulled from the cisterns, drained to the last minute. Someone at the Meridian is hunting for a count that refills.
>
> And the closer Tam gets to the heir she's sworn to hate, the closer she comes to the truth about what lies beneath the clock that hasn't moved in three hundred years.
>
> *A Tithe of Hours is the first book in the Tithebound series: a dark academy romantasy with a slow-burn, enemies-to-lovers romance, a found family worth bleeding for, and a magic system where every spell costs someone their life.*

## Content step

- **Manuscript:** upload the EPUB I'll generate when the book is done.

- **Cover:** a 1600 × 2560 JPG. I can make a type-only cover, or you can use KDP's free Cover Creator. A professional-looking cover matters a lot in this genre.
- **AI disclosure — required.** KDP asks whether AI tools were used to create the text, images or translations. Answer **Yes** for the text, and pick the option that matches how much you edited it. If the cover uses AI images, say yes for images too. Hiding it puts the account at risk.

## Pricing step

- **KDP Select (Kindle Unlimited): enroll.** It means the ebook is exclusive to Amazon for 90-day terms, but you're paid for every page KU readers read (about $0.0048 a page lately; Amazon counts a 95,000-word novel at roughly 450–550 pages, so a full read pays about $2–2.50).
- **Territories:** worldwide.
- **Royalty:** 70% (requires a price from $2.99 to $9.99).
- **Price:** $3.99. Optional launch week at $0.99 (35% royalty) to build early rankings, then back to $3.99.
- **Pre-orders:** skip for Book 1. If a pre-order's final file is late, KDP blocks pre-orders on the account for a year.

## Upload, step by step (about 20 minutes)

**Files:** `dist/book-1-a-tithe-of-hours.epub` (manuscript) and `dist/cover.jpg` (cover, 1600 × 2560). Rebuild both with your pen name first. The current ones say "Pen Name".

1. Go to kdp.amazon.com, sign in, and choose **+ Create → Kindle eBook**.
2. **Kindle eBook Details:** fill in language, title, series (The Tithebound, #1), author (your pen name), the description above, publishing rights, keywords and categories. Leave pre-order off. Click **Save and Continue**.
3. **Kindle eBook Content:**
   - Manuscript: upload the `.epub`.
   - Cover: choose "Upload a cover you already have" and upload `cover.jpg`.
   - AI-generated content: answer **Yes** for text. Say **No** for the cover images, because the cover is type and a drawn clock, made without an AI image generator.
   - Click **Launch Previewer** and page through a few chapters, the contents list and the last page.
   - Click **Save and Continue**.
4. **Kindle eBook Pricing:** enroll in KDP Select, choose all territories and the 70% royalty, and set $3.99 (or $0.99 for launch week). Click **Publish Your Kindle eBook**.
5. Amazon reviews it, which usually takes a few hours and can take up to 72. You'll get an email when it's live.
